About me

A little context on who I am

Hey! I'm Dhiraj Kumar — a Windows Systems Administrator with over 4.6 years of experience at Capgemini, specializing in designing, managing, and optimizing enterprise-scale Windows infrastructure environments.

I care deeply about reliability, security, and operational excellence — not just keeping systems running, but ensuring they perform efficiently, remain compliant, and scale seamlessly with business needs. My expertise spans Windows Server administration (2003–2025), Active Directory, VMware, Citrix, IBM BigFix patch management, and ITIL-based incident and change management.

Throughout my career, I've worked in complex enterprise environments where high availability and proactive problem-solving are critical. I take ownership of the systems I manage, focusing on minimizing downtime, strengthening security, and delivering a consistent experience for users and stakeholders alike.

Outside of my day-to-day responsibilities, I enjoy staying current with emerging technologies, exploring infrastructure automation, and continuously expanding my knowledge of enterprise systems. I believe the best IT professionals never stop learning — and I strive to bring that mindset to every challenge I take on.

Infrastructure-first mindset
I prioritize reliability and security at every layer — from bare metal to virtualized workloads and cloud-connected environments.
Compliance & security driven
I manage patch compliance, vulnerability remediation, and auditing to keep enterprise systems secure and audit-ready at all times.
Continuous learner
